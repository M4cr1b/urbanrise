================================================================================
                              DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
================================================================================

The enhanced property details page follows these design principles:

1. HIERARCHY - Important information is larger and more prominent
2. VISUAL INTEREST - Icons and colors guide the eye
3. PROFESSIONALISM - Gradients and shadows create premium feel
4. USABILITY - Sticky elements keep important info accessible
5. RESPONSIVENESS - Design adapts beautifully to any screen size
6. ACCESSIBILITY - Icons paired with text, good contrast ratios
7. PERFORMANCE - CSS-based effects, no JavaScript bloat
8. BRAND CONSISTENCY - Uses your existing color palette

Result: Property details pages now attract customers with top-notch aesthetics
and professional appearance, encouraging them to contact agents.
